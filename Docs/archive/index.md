# Archive

Completed or superseded task records, deliberately parked future-milestone
tickets, and board history live here. Finished implementation plans live in
[`../plans/archive/index.md`](../plans/archive/index.md).

Future MVP tickets can be created here before their milestone becomes active.
They remain `backlog` items on the archive board and use their milestone tag
(for example, `m-2`) until they are restored to the active board.

## Archive Areas

- [Archive Board](board.md)
- [Archived and Parked Task Records](tickets/index.md)
- [Archived Plans](../plans/archive/index.md)
